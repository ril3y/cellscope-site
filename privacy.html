Privacy Policy

CellScope · Effective October 22, 2026

SupportPrivacy

In short: CellScope collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never sends your data anywhere on its own.

What CellScope uses

Bluetooth. CellScope uses Bluetooth only to find and read your battery's management system (BMS). It doesn't connect to any other Bluetooth device unless you open one.

What stays on your device

While connected, CellScope records a history of your battery (voltage, current, charge level, temperatures, charge and discharge sessions, and events) so it can show charts. This history is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and nobody else, including the developer, can see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you enter a Victron encryption key, it is saved in the same on-device storage and used only to decode that device's broadcasts.

What we collect

Nothing. CellScope includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and makes no network requests in the background.

Reporting an unsupported battery

If you tap Battery not listed? Help add it, CellScope opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub in your browser. It contains the brand, model and notes you typed, the battery's Bluetooth details (advertised name, device ID, services and manufacturer data), and your device's operating system version. Nothing is sent until you review it and submit it yourself on GitHub. Submitted issues are public and covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

CellScope collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
