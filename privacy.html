Privacy Policy

CellScope · Effective October 22, 2026

SupportPrivacy

In short: CellScope collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet.

What CellScope uses

Bluetooth. CellScope uses Bluetooth only to find and read your battery's management system (BMS). It doesn't connect to any other Bluetooth device unless you open one.

What stays on your device

While connected, CellScope records a history of your battery (voltage, current, charge level, temperatures, charge and discharge sessions, and events) so it can show charts. This history is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and nobody else, including the developer, can see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. CellScope makes no network requests and includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Children

CellScope collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
