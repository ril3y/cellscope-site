Troubleshooting

My battery doesn't appear. A battery accepts only one Bluetooth connection at a time. Close the manufacturer's app (and disconnect any other phone), then scan again. Make sure Bluetooth access is allowed for CellScope in Settings.

History is empty. Most batteries keep no log of their own, only lifetime totals. CellScope records history on your device while it's connected, so charts fill in over time. Batteries that do keep a log (ECO) are imported automatically.

Health looks too high. The battery's own health figure is an estimate. For a measured value, run the capacity test on the Test tab: charge to full, then discharge with a steady load until the BMS cuts off.

Does CellScope change my battery?

CellScope only reads your battery. The one exception: on batteries with a clock, it can set the time, and only when you tap to confirm.
